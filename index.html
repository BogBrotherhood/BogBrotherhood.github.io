Who we are

Swamp Panthers is a tight-knit small-gang and black-ops crew. Relaxed on comms. Serious when something lights up. The corp has been flying together since 2013, when we started hunting western Curse. That same group is still here, still looking for a fight.

We gate camp, bubble camp, raid skyhooks, roam looking for trouble, and drop black ops when the target is worth it. Most of the clock is spent hunting.

Primary timezone is USTZ. Ops Corp covers EUTZ.

Where we live

Home is Curse. We hunt and make ISK in Outer Ring, and we PVP through the regions around both.

Industry is part of the swamp, not an afterthought. We keep Athanors and Raitarus in ice-belt systems so you can mine, refine, and build ships where we actually live. Bring a hunter, a builder, or both.
